# **5\. Brand Voice & Visual Direction**

## **5.1 Tone of Voice**

- Warm, plain-spoken, and specific — avoid vague reassurance ("we care about you") in favor of concrete proof (verification steps, named coordinators, transparent process).
- Confident but not corporate: Pioneer Trio should read as modern and approachable, not as a formal, dated agency.
- Bilingual-ready: primary copy in English with Bengali-language content (guides, key page summaries) planned for the Resources section and priority pages, per the business plan's content strategy.
- Consistent across both services: the same trustworthy voice carries through Immigration Consultancy and Marriage Consultancy, even though the emotional register differs (practical/administrative vs. personal/aspirational).

## **5.2 A Note on the Marriage Consultancy Marketing Voice**

The business plan's social-media strategy for the matchmaking service uses an anime/K-drama-inspired "meme mascot" aesthetic (original, firm-owned illustrated characters) to reach the 20–35 audience on Facebook/Instagram/TikTok. That voice belongs on social content and campaign landing pages — the core Marriage Consultancy website page itself should stay closer to the trustworthy, verified tone in 5.1, with the mascot characters appearing as a lighter brand accent (e.g., in illustrations or a footer element) rather than driving the primary service copy.

## **5.3 Logo & Brand Mark**

The approved mark is a three-petal icon — three rounded, leaf-like shapes meeting at a center point to form a balanced, rounded triangle — paired with the wordmark "PIONEER TRIO" in a bold, tracked-out sans-serif. The three petals are a literal, memorable nod to "Trio" and hold up cleanly at small sizes, which matters for a mobile-first, social-heavy launch.

- Primary lockup (icon + wordmark) for the site header, footer, and email signatures.
- Icon-only mark for the favicon, social profile avatars, and app-style touchpoints, since the petals stay legible even at 16–32px.
- Maintain clear space around the mark equal to roughly the width of one petal, and never stretch, recolor, or rearrange the three petals.

## **5.4 Color Palette**

The three petal colors give the site a natural, built-in way to color-code its two Phase 1 services without inventing a separate palette — exactly the "differentiated but related" structure a multi-service site needs:

- **Teal (shared brand) —** the mark's top petal. Used for the logo, main navigation, footer, section headers, and any page or element common to both services (Home, About, Contact, Trust & Safety).
- **Blue —** the mark's left petal, reserved exclusively for Immigration Consultancy: its nav highlight, page accents, icons, and CTA buttons.
- **Pink —** the mark's right petal, reserved exclusively for Marriage Consultancy: its nav highlight, page accents, icons, and CTA buttons.
- Forward-looking note: this leaves the palette ready for Phase 2. The mark already has exactly three petals for three eventual service lines — when the Caregiver Marketplace (business plan Section 5.3) launches, it can take its own accent color within this same teal-anchored system, keeping "one color per service" consistent with the logo's three petals.
- Real, verification-forward imagery over stock-photo cliché: checklists, document icons, verified badges, and (once available, with consent) real success-story photos.
- Mobile-first layout: the business plan specifies a mobile-responsive web application, and Facebook/YouTube are the dominant referral platforms for this audience in Bangladesh.

## **6.4 Marriage Consultancy**

### **Overview**

Pioneer Trio is a verified matchmaking service for Bangladeshi clients in Bangladesh and across the diaspora. You build a verified profile, complete a structured compatibility questionnaire, and are matched by a real human matchmaker — supported by our platform's tools, never replaced by them. Every match is grounded in genuine relationship intent, verified identity, and family-context compatibility.

### **Who This Is For**

Bangladeshi singles, roughly 20–35, in Bangladesh or in the diaspora (UAE, Saudi Arabia, Malaysia, UK, US, Canada, and beyond), who are serious about finding a spouse within a shared cultural and family context — not a casual dating app, and not a traditional broker.

### **How It Works**

- 1\. Complete identity verification and a structured profile covering background, values, family expectations, and relationship goals.
- 2\. Take part in an individual intake interview, which screens for genuine relationship intent.
- 3\. Your matchmaker reviews the client pool and proposes 2–3 candidate matches, with rationale — accounting for cross-border logistics where relevant.
- 4\. A guided first introduction (video call for cross-border matches), followed by matchmaker check-ins with both sides.
- 5\. If both sides want to proceed, optional relationship-readiness consultation sessions are available before family or marriage discussions begin.
- 6\. Your case closes on a successful match commitment, or you remain active in the pool with periodic new-match reviews.

### **Verified & Safe by Design**

- Identity (ID/passport) verification for every profile.
- A mandatory individual interview before any cross-border match proceeds.
- Active screening against status-motivated or coercive intent — see our full Trust & Safety page.
- A named human matchmaker on every case, not an algorithm alone.

### **Why Choose Pioneer Trio**

- Verified profiles and a transparent process — no hidden broker fees.
- A modern, culturally fluent brand that speaks to its audience, not a dated matrimonial-site tone.
- Purpose-built for serious, marriage-intent matching within a shared cultural and family context — not a general dating app.
- Support for both domestic Bangladeshi clients and the global diaspora.

_Required disclaimer copy: "Pioneer Trio is a genuine matchmaking and relationship-readiness service. We do not solicit, screen for, or accommodate marriages sought purely for immigration status in any country, and we actively screen against trafficking risk. Any file showing status-motivated or coercive intent is declined and documented."_

### **FAQ (starter set)**

- How are profiles verified? — Every client completes ID/passport verification and an individual intake interview before joining the active pool.
- Can I use this service if I already have a visa or plan to move abroad? — Yes, provided your interest in marriage is genuine; we do not arrange matches intended solely to secure immigration status.

A dedicated page consolidating every safeguard described in the business plan, since trust is the core differentiator for both services.

All identity documents, immigration case files, and matchmaking profiles are held in encrypted storage with role-based access, so only your assigned coordinator or matchmaker can see your file. Full detail is provided in our Privacy Policy.

| case_qc_review               | id (PK), case_id (FK), prepared_by, prepared_date, reviewed_by, reviewed_date, checklist_complete (bool), final_status, qc_comments                           | Matrix Sections H–I: application QC and four-eyes review           |
| submission_tracking          | id (PK), case_id (FK), application_submitted (bool), submission_date, application_number, biometrics_status, final_decision, case_closed_by, case_closed_date | Matrix "Office Use Only" section                                   |
| client_declarations          | id (PK), case_id (FK), declaration_type, declaration_text, e_signature, declared_at                                                                           | Matrix Section J and the form's Section H consent checkboxes       |
| referral_partners            | partner_id (PK), name, country, license_number, contact_info                                                                                                  | Licensed professionals directory, Section 3.1 of the business plan |
| case_referrals               | id (PK), case_id (FK), partner_id (FK), referred_at, referral_fee, status                                                                                     | Join table: a case may be referred to one or more partners         |
| case_notes                   | id (PK), case_id (FK), coordinator_id, note, created_at                                                                                                       | General coordinator activity log                                   |

Relationships: one client → many cases; one case → many documents, one financial profile, one employment/business profile, many travel-history rows, many home-country-tie rows, and (via case_referrals) many referral partners. case_qc_review, submission_tracking, and client_declarations are 1:1 with a case and exist specifically to carry the Document Verification Matrix's own QC, office-use, and declaration sections into the backend, rather than leaving them as paper-only steps.

**Security:** identity documents, financial documents, and uploaded files in the documents table are held in encrypted, access-controlled storage — visible only to the client's assigned coordinator and, for QC, the second reviewer — consistent with Section 6.6 (Trust & Safety) and the Privacy Policy (Section 6.10).

# **8\. Marriage Consultancy — Profile Form & Data Model**

This section specifies the profile form behind the "Begin My Profile" CTA (Section 6.4) and the database it feeds. One rule governs the whole form: a hard cap of two photographs per profile, enforced both in the form and in the database. There is no automated matching feature anywhere in this system — Section 8.2 sets out the human-led process that replaces it.

## **8.1 Profile Form (Frontend)**

Collected after the client's initial inquiry and before the mandatory individual intake interview (business plan Section 5.2; Trust & Safety, Section 6.6).

### **A. Personal & Identity**

- Full name, date of birth, gender, height, blood group (optional), marital status (never married / divorced / widowed).
- Current nationality, current country & city of residence, visa/residency status (for diaspora clients), mother tongue / languages spoken.

**A deliberate omission:** _this form does not include a skin tone / complexion field, a common field on traditional South Asian matrimonial sites. Pioneer Trio leaves it out by design, consistent with the "modern, not dated" positioning in Section 5.1 and to avoid encoding colorism into the platform._

### **B. Family Background**

- Father's occupation, mother's occupation, number of siblings (married/unmarried), family type (nuclear/joint), family's current location, and a free-text family description.

### **C. Education & Profession**

- Highest education level, institution & field of study, current profession/job title.
- Employer and income bracket — both optional, and visible only to the matchmaker, never shown directly to candidate matches.

### **D. Religious & Cultural Background**

- Religion, sect/denomination if relevant, a free-text description of religious practice (self-described, not a rigid scale), and ethnicity/cultural background (optional).

### **E. Lifestyle**

- Dietary preference, smoking/drinking habits, hobbies & interests (free text).

### **F. Partner Preferences**

- Preferred age range, preferred location(s), preferred education/profession level, religious-practice preference, and other free-text must-haves or dealbreakers.

**Important:** these preferences are input to the matchmaker's manual review (Section 8.2), never parameters to an automated filter or search.

### **G. Photos — Maximum Two**

- Photo 1 (required), Photo 2 (optional). Hard cap of two, enforced client-side (the upload widget disables after two) and server-side (see profile_photos in Section 8.3).
- Photos are held in encrypted, access-controlled storage and shown only to verified, matchmaker-approved candidates — never public, never visible during browsing since there is no browsing feature.

### **H. Verification & Consent**

- Government ID/passport upload — private, verification-only, never shown to matches.
- Consent to the mandatory individual intake interview.
- Relationship-intent declaration: "I am seeking a genuine marriage and am not using this service to obtain immigration status for myself or another person."
- General data-processing consent and e-signature.

### **I. Interview Scheduling**

- Preferred contact method and availability windows for the mandatory individual intake interview.

_Required disclaimer copy: "Pioneer Trio is a genuine matchmaking and relationship-readiness service. We do not solicit, screen for, or accommodate marriages sought purely for immigration status in any country, and we actively screen against trafficking risk. Any file showing status-motivated or coercive intent is declined and documented."_

## **8.2 No Automatic Matching — Human-Led Matching Workflow**

Pioneer Trio's platform runs no compatibility algorithm and has no swipe/browse feature. Matching is entirely matchmaker-led, per this workflow (mirrors the business plan's Section 5.2 operating workflow):

- 1\. The client submits the profile form above.
- 2\. A matchmaker reviews the submitted profile and conducts the mandatory individual intake interview (Section 6.6).
- 3\. The matchmaker records structured, private interview notes and internal profiling tags — compatibility factors, red flags, communication style, family expectations — visible only to Pioneer Trio staff, never to the client or other candidates.
- 4\. When a matchmaker identifies a potential fit from the active pool — informed by the form data and the interview notes, not an algorithm — they manually draft a match proposal with a written rationale.
- 5\. The match proposal and both parties' responses are logged for accountability and future reference, but the "search" step itself stays a human judgment call, never a query the client or a machine runs.

_Required disclaimer copy: "Pioneer Trio does not use automated or algorithmic matching. Every match is proposed personally by a Pioneer Trio matchmaker, based on your profile and intake interview."_

## **8.3 Backend Data Model**

The matrimonial data model separates what a client submits from what only staff can see (interview notes, match rationale), so no interview or matching content is ever exposed through a client-facing view.

| matrimonial_profiles | profile_id (PK), client identity fields, dob, gender, height, blood_group, marital_status, religion, education, profession, current_country, current_city, status, created_at | Sections A–E of the form                                                      |
| profile_photos       | photo_id (PK), profile_id (FK), file_reference, display_order (1 or 2 only — CHECK constraint), uploaded_at, verified (bool)                                                  | Section G of the form — max 2 enforced at the database level, not just the UI |

# **12\. Technical Recommendations**

## **12.1 Platform & Stack**

- **Platform:** a single mobile-responsive web application, built on a low-cost managed stack, that expands from a marketing site into the client portal and matchmaker dashboard described in Sections 7–8.
- **Database:** a relational database (e.g., PostgreSQL) is recommended — both the immigration schema (Section 7.2) and the matrimonial schema (Section 8.3) are highly relational, with foreign-key relationships (client → cases → documents; profile → interviews → match proposals) that a relational database enforces directly, including the two-photo cap as a database constraint rather than only a UI rule.
- **Document & photo storage:** encrypted, access-controlled object storage for immigration documents, ID verification uploads, and profile photos — referenced from the database by file_reference rather than stored inline, consistent with Section 6.6.
- **Authentication & roles:** role-based accounts — Admin/Partner, Immigration Coordinator, Matchmaker, and Client — matching the access rules already described in this document: a coordinator sees only their assigned cases, a second reviewer is required for QC sign-off (Section 7.2) and for matchmaking intake review (Section 6.6), and clients see only their own case or profile, never another client's.
- **Payments:** an international payment gateway supporting both BDT and major foreign currencies, registered under Bangladesh Bank's IT/ITES service-export framework — needed once the site takes paid bookings rather than inquiries.
- **CRM / case management:** the coordinator and matchmaker dashboards described in Section 9 are this CRM — there is no need for a separate third-party system if the schemas in Sections 7.2 and 8.3 are implemented directly, since immigration_cases and matrimonial_profiles already carry stage, owner, and status.
- **Analytics:** lead-source and content-performance tracking to identify which destination/case types and content pieces generate the most qualified leads, per the marketing strategy in the business plan.
